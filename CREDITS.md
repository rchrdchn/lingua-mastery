# Credits for Lingua Magna

See [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md) for every dataset, package and license. The live site shows the same list at `/credits/` and the relevant sources on every course page.

Audio: word recordings from Lingua Libre and Shtooka-project speakers on Wikimedia Commons (named on each word page and in `data/audio/credits.json`), English, French, Italian and German sentence recordings from Tatoeba (CC BY / CC BY-SA), Kokoro-82M text-to-speech for Mandarin (v1.1-zh), Spanish, English, Brazilian Portuguese and French (v1.0), a Piper voice for Italian and Coqui TTS Thorsten VITS (CC0 Thorsten-Voice data) for German; details in [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md#audio).

## Share card fonts

- Outfit (ExtraBold and SemiBold, subset), by the Outfit Project Authors, SIL Open Font License 1.1. See `scripts/og-fonts/OFL-Outfit.txt`.
- DejaVu Sans Bold (subset, used for a few pinyin vowels), Bitstream Vera / DejaVu license. See `scripts/og-fonts/LICENSE-DejaVu.txt`.
- Chinese characters on cards are drawn from Hanzi Writer stroke data (hanzi-writer-data, derived from Arphic PL fonts, Arphic Public License), also listed in NOTICE.md.
